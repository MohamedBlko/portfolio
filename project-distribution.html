Distribution and electrical machines

Load estimates, an induction-motor equivalent circuit and transformer connection exercises.

Status

Focus

Tools and methods
Load calculations / Motors / Transformers

Objective

Translate academic electrical-load and machine problems into explicit assumptions, equivalent circuits and calculations.

Engineering work

  • Break down load types and supply assumptions.
  • Study an induction-motor equivalent circuit.
  • Examine three-phase transformer connections and phase relationships.
  • Keep calculation assumptions visible alongside the results.

Outcome

The saved assignments contain calculation steps and circuit figures. Selected figures are presented as coursework examples rather than design packages for a real installation.

Scope and limits

The archived calculations have not been independently revalidated. Equipment ratings in the exercises are problem data, not professional installations designed or commissioned by the author.
